COMMAND

Rules, conduct, and moderation protocol.

CORE RULES

  1. RESPECT OTHER MEMBERS No harassment, targeted hostility, or personal attacks.
  2. KEEP DISCUSSION CIVIL Anime debates are welcome. Starting wars over them is not.
  3. NO SPAM Avoid flooding forums, chats, or club spaces.
  4. NO NSFW / INAPPROPRIATE CONTENT Keep the club suitable for the wider Chess.com community.
  5. NO CHEATING Follow Chess.com's rules regarding fair play.
  6. FOLLOW STAFF DIRECTIONS Staff decisions may be made to protect the club.

MODERATION LADDER

Depending on severity and history, moderation may progress through the following stages.

01
WARN

A formal reminder that a rule has been broken.

02
MUTE

Temporary restriction from participating.

03
BAN

Removal from the club for serious or repeated violations.

STAFF AUTHORITY

Staff may skip stages when a violation is severe enough to justify immediate action.

Moderation is intended to protect the community, not to win arguments.
